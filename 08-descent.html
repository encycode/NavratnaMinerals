3.5 – 7.2 m · Calcium bentonite

Useful clay. We keep it separate.

The calcium layer becomes bleaching earth and feed binder. It never gets mixed into a drilling or pelletizing grade, because that is how a swelling index drifts. Here is what happens to each layer once it leaves the pit.

Step 01

Selective mining

Each bench is worked by grade, with face samples tested before a truck is loaded.

Step 02

Yard blending

Lots are combined to hit the order's swelling index and methylene-blue value.

Step 03

Drying & activation

Rotary drying to 10–12% moisture; soda-ash or acid activation by grade.

Step 04

Milling

Raymond and micronising mills to 200, 325 or 400 mesh, checked on line.

Step 05

Packing

50 kg bags, jumbo bags or bulk liners, each lot with a COA and retained sample.

You have reached the pay zone

This 3.8-metre horizon is why we are in Kutch. High-swell sodium montmorillonite, worked separately and blended to order. Everything below is milled from it or from the layers we kept apart above.
