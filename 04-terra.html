Why buyers stay

Three things we do not outsource

The mine

Four leases we hold ourselves. No trading desk between the face and your bag, so when a grade shifts we know why and we can say so.

The lab

Swelling index, API viscosity and filtrate loss, methylene blue, GCS and sieve analysis — run in house, twice per lot, with samples retained for twelve months.

The loading

Our own fleet to Mundra and Kandla, 1,500 MT a day, with container stuffing at the plant so nothing waits in a third-party yard.

Mine to vessel

Five stages, each with a hold point

01

Selective mining

Benches worked by grade, with face sampling before dispatch.

02

Yard blending

Lots combined to a target swell and methylene-blue value.

03

Drying & activation

Rotary drying to 10–12%; soda or acid activation by grade.

04

Milling

Raymond and micronising mills to 200, 325 or 400 mesh.

05

Packing

Bags, jumbos or bulk, each with a COA and retained sample.

Reach

Two deep-water ports inside an hour

The plant sits between Mundra and Kandla, which is the quiet advantage of being in Kutch: containers stuffed at the plant in the morning are alongside the same day. Inland, we move by rail rake to Delhi NCR, Pune and the eastern steel belt.
