§02 — Resource

Four leases on the Kutch bentonite belt

The Kutch basin carries some of the highest-swelling sodium bentonite in India. We work four leases by bench, keep the high-swell horizon separate from the calcium clay above it, and blend to specification in the yard rather than hoping the face delivers it.

§03

Mine to vessel

Six stages with a hold point on each. Nothing moves forward on assumption.

Stage 01

Selective mining

Benches worked by grade with face sampling before anything leaves the pit.

Stage 02

Stockyard blending

Lots combined to a target swelling index, methylene-blue value and grit ceiling.

Stage 03

Drying

Rotary dryers bring moisture to 10–12% without over-firing the clay structure.

Stage 04

Activation

Soda-ash activation for sodium grades; acid activation for bleaching earth.

Stage 05

Milling

Raymond and micronising mills to 200, 325 or 400 mesh, checked on-line.

Stage 06

Packing & COA

50 kg bags, jumbo bags or bulk liners, sealed and marked, with a retained sample.
