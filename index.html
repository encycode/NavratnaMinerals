FAQ

What buyers ask first

What is your minimum order quantity?

Trial lots start at 20 MT in bags. Commercial container orders are typically 25–28 MT per 20-foot container; bulk vessel parcels start at 3,000 MT. Mixed-grade containers are possible where packing allows.

Can you match a specification from our current supplier?

Usually, yes. Send the specification sheet and, if possible, a sample. We blend to the target swelling index, viscosity and mesh, send a matched sample for approval, and only then confirm the order.

Do you supply against API 13A?

Our drilling grade is produced to API 13A Section 9, with OCMA grade available. We can arrange third-party verification through SGS or Intertek at the buyer's cost.

What packing options are available?

50 kg HDPE or paper bags, 1.0–1.5 MT jumbo bags with top and bottom spouts, or loose bulk in container liners. Private labelling and multilingual bag printing are available.

What are your payment terms?

Advance TT or irrevocable LC at sight for first orders, with terms reviewed after a shipping history is established. FOB, CFR and CIF quotations are all available.

Send us a specification. We will send a sample.

Grade, tonnage and destination port is enough to start. You get a data sheet, a dispatch date and a price within one working day.
