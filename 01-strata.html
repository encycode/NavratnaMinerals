03 — Applications

Where it goes to work

Drilling & HDD

Viscosity, filtration control and hole stability in water-well, piling and trenchless work.

Foundry & casting

Green-sand bond strength, mould permeability and reduced sand-to-metal defects.

Iron-ore pelletizing

Green-ball formation, drop strength and consistent moisture through the balling drum.

Edible-oil refining

Colour and phospholipid removal with low oil retention on the spent cake.

Civil sealing

Ponds, canals, landfill liners and bentonite-cement cut-off walls.

Ceramics & paper

Plasticity in bodies and glazes; retention and filler performance in paper.

Feed & agriculture

Pellet durability, toxin binding and soil conditioning for arid-zone farming.

Cosmetics & pharma

Low-grit, screened clays for masks, dusting powders and suspension aids.

04 — Mine to vessel

Five controlled stages

Each stage carries its own hold point. Material does not move forward until the previous stage signs off.

Stage 01

Selective mining

Benches worked by grade, with face sampling before dispatch to the stockyard.

Stage 02

Blending yard

Lots blended to a target swelling index and methylene-blue value.

Stage 03

Drying & activation

Rotary drying to 10–12% moisture; soda ash activation where the grade calls for it.

Stage 04

Milling & screening

Raymond and micronising mills to 200, 325 or 400 mesh with on-line sieve checks.

Stage 05

Packing & shipment

Bagged or bulk, sealed and marked, with COA and retained sample per lot.
