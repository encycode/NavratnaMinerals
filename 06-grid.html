Process

Five stages, five hold points

01

Selective mining

Benches worked by grade with face sampling before dispatch.

02

Yard blending

Lots combined to a target swell and methylene-blue value.

03

Drying & activation

Rotary drying to 10–12%; soda or acid activation by grade.

04

Milling

Raymond and micronising mills to 200, 325 or 400 mesh.

05

Packing & COA

Bags, jumbos or bulk liners with a retained sample per lot.
