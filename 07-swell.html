The swelling index test · ASTM D5890

Two grams of clay. One hundred millilitres of water.

This is the test every buyer asks about. Scroll to run it. We do it on every lot before it is bagged.

01

Sprinkle

2 g of dried, milled bentonite goes into the cylinder 0.1 g at a time, so each grain hydrates on its own.

02

Hydrate

Water works its way between the clay layers. The grains open up and the gel at the bottom climbs.

03

Read

After 16 hours we read the settled volume. Drilling grade must reach 24 ml; ours typically reads 30.

Uses

Where swelling does the work

Pond & landfill sealing

Swollen clay closes its own pores. Water stops at the liner.

Drilling & HDD

Gel strength holds the hole open and carries cuttings to surface.

Iron-ore pelletizing

A little binder gives green balls the strength to survive the drop.

Edible-oil refining

Activated clay pulls colour and gums from crude oil.
