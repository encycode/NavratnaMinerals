The line, station by station

Station 01 / 07 · Pit
01

Pit

Benches worked by grade. Face samples tested before a truck is loaded.

4 leases · 9 m pay zone
02

Stockyard

Lots blended to the order's swelling index and methylene-blue value.

Target swell ±1 ml
03

Dryer

Rotary drying to 10–12% moisture without over-firing the clay.

Moisture out ≤ 10%
04

Mill

Raymond and micronising mills grind to 200, 325 or 400 mesh.

On-line sieve every 30 min
05

Bagging

50 kg bags, 1–1.5 MT jumbos or bulk liners. Every lot gets a COA.

1,500 MT a day
06

Truck

Our own fleet. Containers stuffed at the plant, alongside the same day.

62 km · 90 minutes
07

Vessel

Mundra for containers, Kandla for break-bulk. Twenty-four markets served.
